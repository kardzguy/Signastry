The problem

The hobby has a trust problem

Every collector has felt it. You find the piece you've been hunting, and then the doubt sets in. Is the signature real? Will anyone believe it when you sell?

Forgeries

Fakes are everywhere

Forged signatures circulate on every marketplace, and they keep getting harder to spot.

Paper certificates

A COA is only an opinion

Third-party certificates vary in quality, can be faked themselves, and rarely involve the signer at all.

Lost history

Provenance disappears

Receipts, photos and paperwork get separated from the item, and its value goes with them.

Scattered records

Collections live everywhere

Spreadsheets, shoeboxes and phone photos. No single record of what you own.

Risky trades

Buying blind

Sellers can't prove authenticity and buyers can't verify it. Both sides take the risk.

What we're solving

We go to the source

Instead of asking a third party to guess, Signastry submits your item to the signer for confirmation. The result is attached to that item's record in your vault and travels with it when it changes hands. One trusted record per item, from first entry to every future owner.
